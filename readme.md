1. Common Interference Forms:
\tAccording to the source of interference. The interferences received by the satellite navigation positioning receiver can be divided into two categories: man-made interference and non-man-made interference. The main manifestation is the interference of other radio devices from the ground or space to the satellite navigation signals; Man-made interference is a form of malicious interference. Generated by a dedicated interference source. Make the receiver of the other party unable to normally capture, track or erroneously capture satellite navigation signals. Consequently, it aims to prevent the proper positioning.The main thing is to suppress human interference. From the perspective of interference technology system, man-made interference can be divided into . Pressure and deception interference. Pressure interference transmits a certain level of interference signal to mask the useful satellite navigation signals. The signal-to-interference ratio of the satellite navigation signals in the receiver is greatly reduced, and it cannot be normally received and processed. The specific form of suppression interference is diverse. According to the relative size of the spectrum width of interference and the spectrum width of satellite navigation signal, interference can be divided into narrow-band interference and broad-band interference; Deceptive jamming transmits a certain level of erroneous navigation signal to cause the receiver to give out incorrect positioning and timing results. According to the relative size of the spectrum width of interference and the spectrum width of satellite navigation signal, interference can be divided into two types: generational interference and transposition interference.
2. Interference Reduction Method:
\t\tDry-stopping suppression technology is mainly used in DS systems. The types of interference signals it can handle include natural interference, Human interference, Interference from multiple access and multipath interference, etc. The principle of DSSS is to spread the uncorrelated interference signal energy by using the correlation of spreading code in despreading. Concentrate the relevant useful signal energy. But if the interference is too strong. Especially under conditions of hostile interference. The interference intensity can easily exceed the interference tolerance. It is necessary to take other technologies to enhance the anti-interference capability of DSSS system. Interference suppression technology. The basic idea of interference suppression is to take measures to eliminate the strong interference energy before despreading the DSSS signal. Do not allow strong interference to enter the de-spreading and demodulator. Avoid interference beyond the DSSS system's interference tolerance.Interference suppression technology includes interference detection and interference elimination. Interference detection must accurately determine when and where interference exists. Quick Accurately detects interference; Interference elimination requires large processing gain. The damage to the signal is minimal. From the perspective of implementation, The interference suppression algorithm should be as simple as possible. Low computational load. The difficulty of interference suppression technology at present lies in the difficulty of quickly and accurately detecting or estimating how to reduce the damage to the signal when interfering and eliminating interference. Particularly for rapidly changing complex interference. Currently, common interference suppression techniques are divided into three categories: time-domain interference suppression, frequency-domain interference suppression, and spatial domain interference suppression. Domain-transform interference suppression Airspace interference suppression. The time-domain interference suppression technology mainly detects and eliminates interference in the time domain. Including linear prediction, Nonlinear prediction and interference suppression technology assisted by spread spectrum code.The technology of interference suppression in the transform domain mainly detects and eliminates the interference signals in the transform domain. Including based on DFT, Fractional Fourier Transform Wavelet Transform Frequency analysis and other interference suppression technologies. Airspace interference suppression technology mainly refers to the interference suppression technology based on adaptive antenna zeroing.
